# Learn everything

This is my personal Obsidian vault for documenting everything I have learned, am currently learning, and plan to learn.
## What you'll find here

- Things I have learned and understood
- Notes from things I am currently studying
- Concepts, ideas, and useful references
- Projects and experiments
- Problems I have encountered and their solutions
- Things I want to learn in the future
- Learning roadmaps and plans
- Resources worth revisiting
- Personal observations and lessons learned

## Philosophy

This vault is not meant to be a collection of perfectly written notes.
It is a record of my learning process.

## Status

This vault is continuously evolving.

Some notes are complete.  
Some are WIP.  
Some are just ideas for future learning.

That is intentional.
